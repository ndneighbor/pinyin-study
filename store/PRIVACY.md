# Pinyin Study privacy policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Pinyin Study is a Chrome extension by Angelo Saraceno that helps users study Chinese pronunciation, word meanings, and stroke order.

## Local processing
Selected or pasted text is processed on your device using dictionaries and code included with the extension. The extension does not send this text to the developer or third parties. It does not save a lookup history or collect browsing history, account information, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

## Stored preferences
The extension stores your Show meaning preference in local browser storage. It remains on your device and is not transmitted by the extension. Removing the extension clears its local extension storage.

## Website access
The extension detects text selections on ordinary webpages to show pronunciation. Its right-click menu receives the text you select for lookup. This access supports the lookup features; it is not used for tracking.

## External links
The credits contain links to dictionary and licensing websites. Opening one visits that third-party website, which operates under its own privacy policy. Lookup text is not included in those links.

## Data sharing
Pinyin Study does not sell or share user data and does not use it for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.
